Privacy Policy

PM — Shop Now, Pay in Instalments
Effective Date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PM Marketing Hub ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you use the PM mobile application ("the App"). By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. This policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions and Instalment Agreement.

Summary: PM is a shopping app that lets you buy products outright or in instalments. To open an account, verify your identity, assess instalment eligibility, take payments and deliver your order, we collect personal information such as your name, contact details, address, BVN/NIN, employment and guarantor details, bank details, and your location when you choose to use it for delivery. Card payments are processed by Paystack; we do not store your full card number or CVV. We do not sell your personal data. The App does not show ads and does not use third-party analytics.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

  • First name, last name, email address, phone number and password.
  • One-time passcodes (OTPs) sent to your email to verify your account or reset your password.

B. Identity & Verification Information (Verification Centre)

To qualify for instalment purchases, you will be asked to complete your profile in the Verification Centre:

  • Personal information: gender, marital status, home address, city, state, LGA, ward, state of origin, and a photo of a utility bill (with the bill type) as proof of address.
  • Government identifiers: Bank Verification Number (BVN) and National Identification Number (NIN).
  • Employment & income information: employment status, employer or company name, job role, sector and sub-sector, employment history, salary, profit or savings range, workforce size, reason for unemployment (if applicable) and the intended purpose of payment.
  • Guarantor information: your guarantor's name, relationship to you, phone number, email and address. Please make sure you have your guarantor's permission before sharing their details.
  • Bank account: account name, account number and bank.

C. Payment Information

  • Payment cards: when you add a card, we keep only the cardholder name, card brand, bank name, expiry month/year and the last four digits. Your full card number and CVV are not stored on our servers.
  • Payments and wallet: card payments, bank transfers and PM Wallet funding are processed by our payment provider, Paystack, through a secure payment page. We receive the transaction reference, amount and status.
  • Transaction records: orders, instalment plans, payment schedules, payments made, wallet balance and history, and overdue instalments.

D. Orders & Delivery Information

  • Items in your cart, orders, delivery address and phone number, and delivery status.
  • Location: only if you tap the option to use your current location when entering a delivery address, the App reads your device's GPS location once to fill in the address. Location is not collected in the background.

E. Customer Care

Messages, complaints, escalations and email support requests you send us, together with any details you include in them.

F. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Your login session token and App preferences, kept in the App's private storage.
  • Biometric login (optional): if you turn on fingerprint or Face ID login, your login email and password are saved in your device's encrypted secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) so the App can sign you in after your device confirms your fingerprint or face. Your fingerprint or face data never leaves your device and is never accessible to us; the check is performed entirely by your phone's operating system.

G. Information About Delivery and Recovery Staff

The App also contains separate sign-in areas for PM delivery agents and recovery officers. For these users, we process their staff account details, assigned deliveries or recovery tasks, chat messages with the office, photos they take with the camera as proof of delivery or of recovered goods, and the reports they submit. Customers' names, phone numbers, addresses and order details are shown to the assigned staff member only to the extent needed to complete the delivery or recovery.

H. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your contacts, call logs or SMS messages
  • Background or continuous location tracking of customers
  • Microphone recordings
  • Advertising identifiers or third-party analytics / tracking data
  • Your full card number or CVV on our servers

3. Data Storage & Security

Information you submit is sent from the App to our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored on secured servers with access limited to authorised staff who need it to do their jobs. Sensitive login data saved on your phone is kept in the operating system's encrypted secure storage. While we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Paystack processes card payments, bank payments and wallet funding. Your payment details are entered on Paystack's secure page and handled under Paystack's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps / geocoding services (built into Android and iOS) convert your location into a readable address when you choose to use your current location. See Google's Privacy Policy and, on iOS, Apple's privacy policy.
  • Identity verification: your BVN and NIN may be checked with licensed verification providers or the relevant authorities to confirm your identity.

The App does not show ads and does not use third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to communicate with our servers.
  • Location (approximate and precise): only when you choose to fill in your delivery address from your current location.
  • Camera / photos: to take or choose a photo of your utility bill, and for staff to capture proof-of-delivery and recovery photos.
  • Biometrics: for optional fingerprint / Face ID login.
  • Notifications: to show order, delivery and payment alerts.

You can turn off any of these permissions in your device settings at any time. Some features (for example, using your current location or biometric login) will not work without them, but you can still use the rest of the App.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run the App, such as Paystack and identity verification providers, and only for those purposes;
  • With our delivery agents and recovery officers, limited to what they need to complete a delivery or recovery;
  • With your guarantor, where necessary in connection with unpaid instalments, as described in the Instalment Agreement;
  • With law enforcement, regulators, courts or credit bureaus where required by law or to protect our rights, including in cases of fraud or default;
  • With a successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of our business, under the same protections described here.

7. Data Retention

We keep your personal information for as long as your account is active and for as long as needed to provide our services, complete any outstanding instalment plan, and meet our legal, tax, accounting, and anti-fraud obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it. Information saved on your device stays there until you log out, turn off biometric login, clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

8. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information (much of it can be updated directly in your Profile and Verification Centre);
  • Request deletion of your account and personal information;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

To request account or data deletion, email us at the address below from your registered email address. We will confirm your request and respond within 30 days. Please note that if you have an outstanding instalment balance, or if the law requires us to keep certain records (such as transaction records), we may keep that information until the obligation ends.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or services. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and where the change is significant we will notify you in the App. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

This page explains how to request that your PM app account, and the personal data associated with it, is deleted. The PM app is published on Google Play by PM Marketing Hub.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to support@pmstores.com from the email address registered on your PM account, so we can confirm the request comes from you.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my PM account.
  3. In the message, include your full name and the phone number registered on your account.
  4. We may reply with a one-time code or a few questions to verify your identity. Please respond to complete the request.
  5. We will confirm when your account has been deleted. Requests are completed within 30 days.

Email: support@pmstores.com

Email a deletion request
Outstanding instalments: if you have an unpaid instalment balance, your account cannot be deleted until the balance is settled, because the product remains the property of PM Marketing Hub until the final instalment is paid. We will let you know if this applies to you.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and password
  • Profile and verification details: gender, marital status, home address, state of origin, utility bill photo, BVN and NIN
  • Employment and income information
  • Guarantor information
  • Saved bank account and saved payment cards (card brand, last 4 digits, expiry)
  • Saved delivery addresses, cart and notifications
  • Customer care messages

Login details saved on your phone for fingerprint / Face ID login are removed when you log out, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Deleting Some of Your Data Without Deleting Your Account

You can remove saved payment cards, and update your profile and verification details, directly in the app. To ask us to delete other specific information without closing your account, email support@pmstores.com with the subject Data deletion request and tell us what you would like removed.
